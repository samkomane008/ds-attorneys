About the firm

Counsel built around your goals, not our case files.

DS Attorneys is a Pretoria-based commercial law firm led by Director Dondré Samuels, built to give businesses and individuals direct access to sharp, practical legal counsel. Rather than treating clients as case numbers, we invest the time to understand each client's goals and risks, then build a legal strategy around what actually moves their business forward.

Our practice sits at the intersection of traditional commercial law and the industries shaping South Africa's economy today: construction, technology, and cross-sector commercial trade. That combination allows us to advise clients not just on what the law says, but on how it plays out in practice, in their specific industry.

Clients get senior attention on every matter, clear communication throughout, and advice delivered in plain language rather than legal jargon. So decisions can be made with confidence

Our services

Five core practice areas. One standard of counsel.

Commercial & Civil Disputes

We represent businesses and individuals in commercial and civil litigation, working to resolve disputes efficiently while protecting our clients' financial and reputational interests. Every matter is approached with a clear strategy from the outset, so clients understand their position and options at every stage.

Dispute Resolution

Beyond the courtroom, we guide clients through mediation, negotiation, and arbitration to reach faster, cost-effective outcomes. Our focus is on resolving conflict in a way that preserves relationships and value wherever possible, while remaining ready to litigate firmly when required.

Construction Law

We advise contractors, developers, and property owners on the full lifecycle of construction matters, from contract drafting and tender disputes to delay claims and defects liability, helping clients avoid costly project disruptions.

ICT & Technology Law

As technology reshapes how businesses operate, we help clients navigate software licensing, data protection, service level agreements, and technology procurement, ensuring their digital operations are legally sound and future-proof.

Commercial Contracts & Advisories

We draft, review, and negotiate commercial agreements that protect our clients' interests while enabling their business goals, from shareholder agreements to supply and service contracts, backed by clear, practical legal advice.
