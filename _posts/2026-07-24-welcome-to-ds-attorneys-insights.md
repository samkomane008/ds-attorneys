Welcome to Insights, the section of our website where we share practical commentary on the legal issues that affect South African businesses day to day.

Our aim here is simple. Legal information is easy to find but hard to trust, and most of what business owners read online is either too general to be useful or written for other lawyers. We want to close that gap with clear, honest writing on the questions clients actually ask us.

## What we will cover

Expect short, readable pieces on the areas we work in every day:

- Commercial and civil disputes, and how to avoid the ones that are avoidable
- Contracts, including the clauses that quietly cause the most trouble later
- Dispute resolution, and when litigation is and is not the right tool
- Construction law and the practical realities of project disputes
- Technology and ICT law, from licensing to data protection

## How to use this

Nothing here is legal advice for your specific situation. Every matter turns on its own facts, and a short article cannot replace a proper consultation. What these pieces can do is help you spot issues earlier, ask better questions, and understand the shape of a problem before it grows.

If something you read raises a question about your own business, that is exactly the right moment to talk to us.
